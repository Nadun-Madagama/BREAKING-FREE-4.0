Myths vs Reality

Myth: "It reduces stress and is harmless."

Reality: It physically alters the brain, damages health, and creates immense long-term stress.

Myth: "Everyone is doing it."

Reality: You only see what's visible. The majority live healthy, meaningful lives without substances.
