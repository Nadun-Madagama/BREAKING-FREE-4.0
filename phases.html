Our Mission & Journey

Discover our vision and the structured path we take from awareness to reintegration.

A Movement, Not Just a Program

Project Breaking Free 4.0 is about tearing down the walls of stigma and building bridges to a better future. We combine modern psychological practices with community support to ensure long-term rehabilitation and reintegration.

Our holistic approach focuses not just on recovery, but on rediscovery of self-worth and purpose.

  • Our Mission

    To provide a second chance through education, skill-building, and emotional support.

  • Our Vision

    A society where every individual has the opportunity to rewrite their story.

  • Core Values

    Empathy, Integrity, Resilience, and Community.

Phase 02: Rehabilitation & Recovery

“Say No Today For A Better Tomorrow”

A meaningful and inspiring initiative designed to bring hope, motivation, and a renewed sense of purpose to individuals on their recovery journey.

Awrudu Uthsawaya & Motivational Programme

Phase 02

Date

18th of April 2026

Location

Rehabilitation Center, Rathnapura

“Rediscovering Joy Without Drugs.”

The day began with a vibrant Awrudu Uthsawaya, where participants engaged in a series of fun-filled traditional games and activities. Laughter, teamwork, and excitement filled the environment as everyone took part with great enthusiasm.

This celebration carried a powerfully resonant message—life can be joyful, exciting, and absolutely fulfilling without the need for drugs. Through these seemingly simple yet highly meaningful moments, participants experienced how genuine, long-term happiness can come naturally from connection, culture, and shared experiences.

A key highlight of the day was the dedicated motivational session, designed to inspire and empower individuals currently undergoing rehabilitation. The session deeply focused on resilience, discovering self-belief, and understanding the profound importance of making positive life choices.

What made this session truly impactful was the direct involvement of individuals who had successfully completed their own rehabilitation and are now leading stable, successful, and happy lives. Their personal stories bridged the gap between the past and the future—highlighting the struggles faced during addiction, the incredible strength required to overcome them, and the ultimate success achieved.

These powerful narratives served as living proof that complete transformation is not just a dream, but an achievable reality, creating a deep emotional connection and inspiring current participants to deeply believe in their own potential.

As part of our ongoing commitment, we concluded the phase by making a donation of essential items to the center, aiming to substantially support their daily needs and sustainably improve the overall well-being of the residents.

Phase 03: Youth Awareness & Community Action

“Empower Youth. Protect the Future.”

This phase is dedicated to engaging and empowering youth to take a stand against drug abuse. By reaching young individuals directly within the community, we inspire responsible choices and a united movement toward a drug-free generation.

Leaflet & Sticker Distribution

Session 01

Date

1st of April 2026

Time

9.00 AM Onwards

Location

Kiribathgoda Town

“Youth Against Drugs. Be the Change.”

As the opening initiative of Phase 03, this campaign took awareness directly to the streets—connecting with youth in real-world spaces. Through impactful leaflets and bold stickers, we delivered a clear message: the power to say no lies within you.

Each interaction sparked meaningful conversations, and every sticker distributed acted as a silent advocate, building a stronger, more informed youth community ready to stand together against drug abuse.
